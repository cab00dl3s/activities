Race Assessment

▲ Strengths

+Robust aerobic base. 415km run + 376km hiked in 12 months. Long-duration cardiovascular capacity is well established.
+Mountain legs from summer 2025. 11 days of alpine hiking, up to 1,799m D+ per day. Your legs understand sustained steep ascent.
+Descent pace is elite. Confirmed 5:30–6:00/km downhill. The 5km descent from km 15 banks 28+ minutes on anyone hiking it.
+Sharp flat speed in taper week. Zwift Jun 8 & 10: 5:41–5:59/km at HR 152 — the engine is primed.
+Controlled HR under load. Long efforts consistently HR 110–155 — aerobic zone, not redlining.
+Rock climbing proprioception. Technical terrain confidence translates directly to Dolomite rocky trails.

▼ Risks & Watch Points

!Limited run-specific vertical. Only 452m total D+ in May runs. Trail legs behave differently from flat legs on 1,000m of race climbing.
!Longest trail run is 12.6km. The race is 20km — the final 7km at altitude will be uncharted fatigue territory. Run conservative in the first half.
!Altitude at Cortina (1,224m+). Peaks reach ~2,100m. Expect 5–8% reduced oxygen delivery without prior acclimatisation — build pace buffer in.
!17:00 start → evening running. Temperature drops post-sunset during the descent. Pack mandatory 500ml but prepare for cooling conditions km 15–20.
!No poles. Quad-dominant descent without assistance. The 5km downhill at race-end on tired legs will test eccentric strength hard.
